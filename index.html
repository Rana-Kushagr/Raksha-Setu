TECHNIQUE 1: CHEST CPR

Adult CPR Chest Compressions

Lock elbows straight at 90°. Push 2 inches down in center of chest between nipples at 110 beats per minute.

110 BPM RHYTHM • 2 INCH DEPTH (5-6 CM)
Allow chest to recoil fully between compressions
TECHNIQUE 2: BLEEDING STOP

Emergency Tourniquet Windlass

Wrap cloth 2-3 inches above wound, knot with a strong stick, twist until spurting blood stops, lock stick.

TWIST STICK TIGHT UNTIL BLEEDING STOPS
Never place over elbow/knee joints • Note application time
TECHNIQUE 3: CHOKING RESCUE

Heimlich Abdominal Thrusts

Stand behind victim. Place fist just above navel. Pull sharply inward and upward to pop out trapped object.

5 BACK BLOWS + 5 INWARD-UPWARD THRUSTS
Quick upward thrust under diaphragm expels airway obstruction
TECHNIQUE 4: AIRWAY SAFETY

Unconscious Recovery Position

Roll breathing victim onto side. Bend top knee at 90° angle. Rest cheek on back of hand, tilt chin up.

SIDE ROLL PREVENTS TONGUE / VOMIT CHOKING
Keep victim in this posture while awaiting ambulance arrival

Exact Adult CPR Action Guide

  1. 1
    Check Responsiveness & Breathing

    Tap shoulders firmly. Shout "Are you okay?". Look for chest rise for 5 seconds.

  2. 2
    Call 112 on Speakerphone

    Put phone on loudspeaker so hands remain free to do compressions immediately.

  3. 3
    Lock Hands in Center of Chest

    Place heel of palm between nipples. Interlock fingers. Lock elbows completely straight.

  4. 4
    Push Hard & Fast (2 Inches Depth)

    Use your upper body weight. Allow full recoil between pushes. Do not stop until medics arrive.
